
Built-in Diagnostic Navigation Keymaps

• Jump to next error: Press ]e
• Jump to previous error: Press [e
• Jump to next diagnostic (any severity — error, warning, hint): Press ]d
• Jump to previous diagnostic: Press [d
• Jump to next warning: Press ]w
• Jump to previous warning: Press [w

Viewing & Searching Errors

• Show error details under cursor: Press <leader>cd (usually Space + c + d) to open the floating diagnostic popup with the full message.
